Indifference objections to the fine-tuning argument hold that the probability of fine-tuning on theism itself is very low or inscrutable, due to the vast range of alternatives God has for creating a life-permitting universe. We defend this response against a litany of recent objections. First, we develop a Bayesian model showing that one cannot relegate facts about alternative law structures into the background, since the very facts about those structures that make fine-tuning unlikely are themselves unlikely on theism in a way that cancels out any evidence from fine-tuning. Second, we point out that since God’s options for producing a life-permitting universe always exceed the naturalistic possibilities, that we find ourselves in a universe with physical parameters counts as evidence against theism. Third, we show how our model addresses important defenses of the fine-tuning argument.
